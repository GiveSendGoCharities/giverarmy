The questions you're right to ask.

Is this legit?

Yes. GiveSendGo Charities, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) public charity (EIN 88-3776392), audited annually, with Candid's Platinum Seal of Transparency for 2025. Needs are reviewed before any grant is made.

What does "gift #" mean?

It's the running count of gifts to the Giver Army Fund. It counts gifts, not people: a member who gives monthly for two years is 24 gifts.

Is the Giver Army part of GiveSendGo Charities?

Yes. The Giver Army is GiveSendGo Charities' community of monthly givers. Every grant GiveSendGo Charities makes, including Care & Relief and Day One Relief, is the Army showing up.

Where does my gift go?

Into the Giver Army Fund, which is unrestricted. Our grant team sends it to validated needs across twelve cause areas. By our published rolling 12-month estimate, 92% goes to direct grants.VERIFY vs. 990

Can I really start at $5?

Yes. $5 a month is a full membership. You can raise, lower, or cancel anytime from your account.

Is GiveSendGo Charities the same as GiveSendGo.com?

No. GiveSendGo Charities, Inc. is a separate nonprofit. Your Giver Army gift is made to and stewarded by GiveSendGo Charities, not GiveSendGo.com LLC.
